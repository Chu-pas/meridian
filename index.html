Who we are

About us

Meridian was founded in 1992 on a simple idea: banking should keep up with how people actually live, not the other way around. Over three decades, we've grown from a single branch into a full-service bank, while staying focused on what mattered from day one — fast, clear service, without the runaround.

Today, that same philosophy shapes our digital account: fast transfers, clear pricing, and an app that works the way modern banking should — plus everyday checking, savings goals, virtual cards, and bill pay, all from a single account with no hidden fees and no branch visits required.

  • Quarterly performance summaries and growth reporting

  • Long-term partnerships built on stability, not speculation
Doing right

Responsibility

We believe a bank's first responsibility is to the people who trust it with their money. That means lending carefully, being upfront about fees, and never extending credit a customer can't realistically manage.

It also means investing in the communities we serve — through financial literacy resources, support for local small businesses, and a genuine commitment to making banking more accessible, not less.
